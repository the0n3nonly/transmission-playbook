# TRANSMISSION Playbook

The open playbook for running a 48-hour studio session — schedule, roles, artist briefing, and what we learned. From THE TRANSMISSION, November 14–16, 2026.

## What this is

A complete, reusable playbook for an invite-only 48-hour studio session: two days, one room, a roster of artists, and a finished body of work at the end. Everything in here already happened — nothing in this repo is unreleased. No unreleased music, no tracklists, no lyrics. Process only.

## The session

- **When:** November 14–16, 2026 — 48 hours, 8:00 AM to 8:00 AM
- **Where:** Whitestone Recording Studio, Whitestone, NY
- **Format:** Invite-only. Artists welcome both days, encouraged to attend both.
- **Day 1 — KWO (Kult World Order):** build the transmitter.
- **Day 2 — CVA (Cyborgs Vs Aliens):** the broadcast.
- **Sponsored by:** R.I.P KULT

## Roles in the room

- **Artists** — the roster, writing and recording.
- **Engineer** — recording, engineering, producing.
- **Live band** — sax and more, playing live in the room.
- **Photographers / videographers** — on site all 48 hours.
- **Food** — catered on site, so nobody leaves the room hungry.

## The schedule (template)

- **Before:** lock the room, confirm the roster, send the artist briefing, set the day themes.
- **Day 1:** doors open, setup, sessions run around the clock. Day theme: build.
- **Day 2:** sessions continue, day theme: broadcast. Wrap with a finish plan: artists complete recordings at their own studios, one coordinator keeps the pieces moving.
- **After:** collect everything, back it up twice, and set the finish plan — who records what, where, and by when.

## The artist briefing

Every artist got a briefing before the session: the concept, the day themes, what to bring, and how the two days work. The template is in `docs/briefing-template.md`.

## What we learned

- A 48-hour session produces more than the sum of its hours — the second day is where the work gets strange and good.
- Keep the room fed and the cameras rolling; the footage is the proof and the promo.
- The finish plan matters as much as the session: recordings get completed after, at the artists' own studios, with one person coordinating.
- Over 300 GB of footage and audio came out of one weekend. Back it up twice.

## Status

Complete — this session happened. The playbook is open for any artist collective to reuse.
