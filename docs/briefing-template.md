# Artist Briefing Template

A template for the briefing every artist gets before a 48-hour session. Adapt the names, dates, and themes — keep the structure.

## Session

- **Name:** [Session name]
- **Dates:** [Start day, time] → [End day, time] (48 hours)
- **Location:** [Studio name, address]
- **Format:** Invite-only

## The concept

[One paragraph: what the session is, what it's building toward.]

## Day themes

- **Day 1 — [Theme]:** [What the day is for — e.g. build.]
- **Day 2 — [Theme]:** [What the day is for — e.g. broadcast.]

Artists are welcome both days and encouraged to attend both.

## What to bring

- A laptop or a means to record yourself — every artist records their own takes.
- Your ideas, your lyrics, your instrument if you play one.
- Anything you need for 48 hours in one room.

## Schedule

- Doors open and setup.
- Sessions run around the clock, both days.
- Photographers and videographers on site throughout.
- Food catered on site.

## Roles

- **Artists** — writing and recording.
- **Engineer** — recording, engineering, producing.
- **Live band** — playing live in the room.
- **Photo / video** — documenting everything.

## After the session

Recordings get finished after the weekend, at the artists' own studios, with one coordinator keeping the pieces moving. Nothing is rushed out the door — the session is the start, not the release.
